☀ Solar Forecast Parameters (Open-Meteo)
Saved to `solar_forecast_config.json` — automatically calibrated over time as actual PV telemetry is recorded
How to determine your parameters:
• Latitude & Longitude: Open Google Maps, right-click on your house/roof, and click the coordinates at the top of the menu to copy them (e.g. 51.5074, -0.1278). Paste directly into Latitude to auto-fill Longitude.
• Array Capacity (kWp): Total rated DC power of your panels (e.g. 10 × 400W = 4.0 kWp). Check your MCS certificate or inverter paperwork.
• Roof Tilt (°): Angle from horizontal (0° = flat, 90° = vertical). Typical UK pitched roofs are 30°–40° (default 35°).
• Azimuth (°): Compass direction panels face (180° = Due South, 90° = East, 270° = West, 0° = North).
